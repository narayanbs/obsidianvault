# Step-by-step from an Android phone

### Step 1 — Install two apps

On the phone, go to Google Play Store and install:

**1. Jeevan Pramaan**

The official app is published for pensioners and supports Aadhaar Face Authentication. ([Google Play][3])

**2. AadhaarFaceRD**

This is the supporting Aadhaar face-authentication service.

The official Jeevan Pramaan website confirms that **both apps are required for Face Authentication**. ([Jeevan Pramaan][2])

You can start from the official government download page:

[Official Jeevan Pramaan App Download Page](https://jeevanpramaan.gov.in/v2.0/apppackage/appdownload?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Important:** Don't download APKs from random websites. Use the Play Store/official Jeevan Pramaan site.

---

### Step 2 — Open Jeevan Pramaan

When you open it, you will initially see **Operator Authentication**.

This sounds complicated, but **you can be the operator** and help your mother.

The operator can be a family member assisting the pensioner.

You will be asked for:

* Aadhaar number
* Mobile number
* Email address

SBI's published Face Authentication procedure confirms these fields. ([SBI][4])

You then authenticate the operator using the procedure shown by the app.

---

### Step 3 — Enter your mother's pension details

After operator authentication, the app will take you through the pensioner's details.

This is where you need her pension documents.

You should have:

**PPO number**
Her Pension Payment Order number.

**Pension account number**
Her SBI account into which the pension is credited.

**Bank name**
Select **State Bank of India / SBI**.

There will also be fields concerning the pension authority/sanctioning authority and pension type. **Don't guess these fields.** The correct choices depend on what is printed in her PPO/pension records.

The official Jeevan Pramaan system requires information such as Aadhaar number, PPO, bank account, bank name and mobile number. ([Jeevan Pramaan][5])

---

### Step 4 — Face Authentication

This is the important part.

Have your mother sit in front of the phone.

Ideally:

* Sit in a well-lit room.
* Remove sunglasses.
* Keep her face directly in front of the camera.
* Don't wear anything that obscures her face.
* Hold the phone reasonably steady.
* Follow the instructions on the screen.

The app uses the phone camera to perform **Aadhaar-based Face Authentication**. No fingerprint scanner is required. ([Jeevan Pramaan][2])

For an elderly person, I would recommend **you operate the phone while she simply sits in front of it**.

---

## Step 5 — Successful submission

If authentication succeeds, the **Digital Life Certificate (DLC)** is generated.

A unique **Pramaan ID** will be generated, and an acknowledgement SMS is sent to the registered mobile number. ([Google Play][3])

### This is the important part:

**You do NOT need to print the certificate and take it to SBI.**

Jeevan Pramaan stores the certificate electronically, and the pension disbursing agency can access it online. The system also provides electronic delivery to the pension disbursing agency. ([Jeevan Pramaan][5])

So, in the normal successful case:

**Mother → Face authentication → Digital Life Certificate → electronically available to SBI**

No bank visit.

---

# One thing I strongly recommend

You may never need to give it to SBI, but keep it safely in your phone/computer.

The official system allows the certificate to be downloaded using the Jeevan Pramaan ID. ([Jeevan Pramaan][5])
